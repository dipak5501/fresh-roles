      Check for new posts every 10 minutes
      
      Click Refresh new jobs, or run this once in Terminal from this folder. It does not submit applications.
      
      
      python3 app.py refresh
      
      
      To repeat that every 10 minutes, you can add a cron line. Remotive is still queried only a few times a day, and Arbeitnow about once an hour.
      
      
      */10 * * * * cd "/Users/dslc/Try Job hunt" && /usr/bin/python3 app.py refresh >> "/Users/dslc/Try Job hunt/data/refresh.log" 2>&1
